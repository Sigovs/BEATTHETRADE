# index_v2 — "The plate and the ladder"

Alternate direction to `index.html`. Same copy, claims and mechanics; a different visual system.

## Concept
Your trade-in is the floor, not the price. The page opens on the one thing only you have, your license
plate, and turns the site's own example auction into a ladder: the trade-in is the floor, every bid is a
floor above it, and the top-bid figure climbs the floors and crosses your minimum. Green means the price
moved up; orange is only the action.

## Directions weighed
- **A · Plate + ladder (chosen).** Hero = the plate as the input; signature = the price ladder. Every
  figure is real (the site's example auction), and the form never moves.
- **B · Ring of dealers (killed).** A car surrounded by dealer nodes needs dealer positions and counts
  the site never publishes; it would be invented data.
- **C · One afternoon, 1–5 PM (killed as a whole).** Bid timestamps are unknown, so the clock would be
  fiction, and a pinned story delays the proof. Its true part (weekdays 1:00–5:00 PM ET) lives in How it works.

## Principles taken (research/inspiration.md + 2026-10-28 pass)
Cape Town Dam Levels: trust one number by marking its threshold → the reserve is a physical line.
Slash: name the fear, then one field → the plate. StockX: fixed market words (bid, top bid).
Midday: real data rows as the imagery → results as a tape of rows, one featured.
Not taken: dashboards, tickers, dealer networks, luxury cues.

## Rhythm
paper hero (quiet, the plate) → night ladder (dense, signature) → paper lane (calm) → white results
(image + data) → navy quote (one voice) → paper FAQ → night close (the chevron window) → paper footer.

## Type
Archivo variable, one family: wide (≈112%) for statements, echoing the BTT logotype; condensed (≈80%)
for figures; normal for reading. No caps labels.

## Motion
Tier 1: control feedback. Tier 2: headings rise out of word masks, blocks float up once (the approved
Gunther G2 system, triggered, never scrubbed). Tier 3: since the 3/10 review of the ladder, the market section carries the index phone (plate → minimum → live bids → reserve met → sold) with its glass widget callout, sticky beside the chapters; the closing
chevron window rises open. Reduced motion and no-JS show the finished ladder.
