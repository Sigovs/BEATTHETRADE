Questions sellers ask.

All FAQs →
Is it really free?

Yes. Listing your car and running the auction cost you nothing. Dealers pay a fee only when they win.

Who is bidding on my car?

Verified, licensed local dealers. They're notified when your auction goes live.

What if the bids come in low?

You set a minimum. If bidding doesn't reach it, your car doesn't sell. You can change your minimum during the auction, and relist for free if it doesn't sell.

What happens when my minimum is met?

The highest bid at or above your minimum wins, and you complete the sale with that dealer. We connect you with them; they pay you, then pick up the car.

How long does it take?

Listing takes 5–10 minutes. Auctions run weekdays from 1:00 to 5:00 PM ET, and a bid in the last minute adds a minute.

Which cars can I list?

Cars registered in Massachusetts or New Hampshire, built after 1992.
